About us

Royal Traders is a trusted name in the trade of high-quality pulses and spices, committed to delivering purity, freshness, and value in every grain. SERVICES

Our services are built on the pillars of trust, consistency, and customer satisfaction.

Wholesale Trading

We offer a wide range of carefully selected pulses such as chana dal, toor dal, moong dal, urad dal, and masoor dal. Sourced directly from reliable farmers and suppliers, our pulses are known for their quality, freshness, and nutritional value.

Bulk Distribution

We support bulk buyers, retailers, and food industries by supplying large quantities with prompt logistics. Our team ensures on-time delivery and consistent quality, whether you're ordering monthly stock or a one-time bulk consignment.

Custom Packaging

From turmeric and cumin to pepper and coriander, our range of Indian spices brings aroma and flavour to every kitchen. We ensure each spice is cleaned, packed, and delivered to meet the highest quality standards.
